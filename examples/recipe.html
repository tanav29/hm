Lemon & herb pasta

A bright, simple bowl for the days when lunch should feel like a pause.

25 minutes · Serves 2

You'll need

Pasta, one lemon, olive oil, parmesan, a handful of parsley, salt and pepper.

Make it

  1. Cook the pasta in well-salted water; save a cup of the cooking water.
  2. Toss with lemon zest, juice, olive oil and a splash of pasta water.
  3. Finish with parmesan, parsley and plenty of black pepper.
